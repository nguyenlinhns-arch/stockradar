# StockRadar Compliance Review V2

Status: **BLOCKED — formal Vietnamese legal/compliance review not completed**. This is an engineering/product checklist, not legal advice.

## Mode gate

`INTERNAL → RESEARCH_ONLY → COMPLIANCE_REVIEW → PRODUCTION_APPROVED`. Only an authorized approver may promote mode. GitHub Pages is fixed to RESEARCH_ONLY.

## Review scope

- product classification and permitted securities-research/education boundaries;
- whether any recommendation, personalization, alert or paid feature requires authorization;
- marketing and performance-claim substantiation;
- financial-services advertising eligibility and platform rules;
- licensed market/fundamental/news/benchmark/corporate-action data and derived outputs;
- privacy, consent, cookies/analytics, email and retention;
- subscription terms, billing, tax/invoice, refund and consumer disclosures;
- conflicts, corrections, complaints, incident and audit procedures;
- brand/trademark clearance.

## Claim controls

No guaranteed return, score-as-probability, fabricated/live-mislabeled track record, urgency to buy, individualized suitability claim, automatic order implication or concealment of losses/unactivated records. Disclaimers do not cure an otherwise impermissible product or claim.

## Approval evidence

Named reviewer, jurisdiction/scope, reviewed artifact versions, decisions/conditions, unresolved risks, expiry/review date and immutable approval record. Production remains blocked until all conditions, data rights, security, operational and monitoring gates pass.
