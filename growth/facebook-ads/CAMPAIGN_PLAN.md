# Facebook Ads Validation Plan V2 — 3–5 triệu đồng

Status: **HOLD** until data rights, compliance, production measurement and new creative review pass. Nothing in this document means an ad was launched.

## Three first-round propositions

| Ad set | User question | Landing | Daily media | Days |
| --- | --- | --- | ---: | ---: |
| HORIZON TOP | “Top nào phù hợp đúng thời gian nắm giữ của tôi?” | `/radar5` | 150.000đ | 7 |
| TICKER SEARCH | “Mã tôi quan tâm đang ở trạng thái nào?” | `/phan-tich` | 150.000đ | 7 |
| RECOMMENDATION HISTORY | “Khuyến nghị trước đây được tính hiệu quả ra sao?” | `/hieu-qua` | 150.000đ | 7 |

Base media is 3.150.000đ before account-specific tax. Verify the live invoice, policy eligibility and approved cash envelope before launch. Any remaining budget is a reserve, not automatic scale.

Breakout and Risk Radar are retained as product surfaces but **retired from the first V2 acquisition round**. Their V1 raster files are historical assets and must not be uploaded as if they were approved V2 creative.

## Fair test

- same eligible Vietnam audience and placements;
- ad-set budgets so each proposition receives equal spend;
- same launch time and seven-day window;
- landing-page view or verified signup optimization only when the event is reliable;
- two reviewed creative variants per proposition;
- no editing during D0–D3 unless tracking or policy is broken.

## Activation and decision

An activated user completes signup and then performs one high-intent action: changes horizon, searches a ticker, opens a recommendation list, views performance, or opens the full sample report.

Choose by:

1. cost per activated user;
2. D7 retention;
3. D1 retention;
4. email opt-in;
5. Advanced intent;
6. verified payment only after billing is legitimately live.

CTR and CPC diagnose creative; they do not choose the product proposition.

## Mandatory stop conditions

- MOCK/SHADOW is presented as live market output;
- data or creative rights are unresolved;
- Meta or Vietnamese compliance review is incomplete;
- production events are broken or duplicate;
- financial-services authorization is requested but unavailable;
- copy implies guaranteed return, individualized advice, urgency to buy, or score-as-probability;
- spend would exceed the approved envelope.

## Pre-launch sequence

`PRODUCTION_APPROVED mode → licensed current data → compliance sign-off → six new assets → event QA → account/policy verification → simultaneous launch`.
