# Experiment Framework

Unit of comparison: proposition, not individual ad.

Keep constant:

- audience eligibility and geography;
- run window;
- placement policy;
- daily budget;
- signup friction;
- tracking contract.

Vary:

- proposition promise;
- proposition-specific creative/copy;
- matching landing-page content.

Do not merge propositions into one landing page during the first test. Do not move budget to an early CTR leader before minimum observation unless a cell is technically broken.

Every change is appended to `STOCKRADAR_EXPERIMENTS.md` with timestamp, reason and affected cell.
