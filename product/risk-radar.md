# Risk Radar

Job: identify when previously attractive evidence becomes stale, damaged or invalid.

High-value triggers:

- `READY → INVALIDATED`;
- `TRIGGERED → EXTENDED`;
- Market `GREEN/YELLOW → RED`;
- candidate `TOP5 → OUT_OF_TOP5`;
- corporate action, new filing or event invalidates the basis;
- snapshot changes and current price exits the valid plan region.

Risk Radar is the leading retention hypothesis. It is not a hard-stop execution service.
